### Description
```text
Disk Direct turns your own devices — your Mac, Windows or Linux PC, home server, NAS, or OpenWrt router with a USB drive — into a fast, zero-cloud personal workspace connected directly peer-to-peer over encrypted WebRTC channels.

Your files stay on your hardware. There is no third-party cloud storage limit, no central password database, and no middleman holding your data.

KEY FEATURES

• Direct Peer-to-Peer File Access
Browse, stream, upload, organize, and share files across all your paired devices in real time over encrypted WebRTC DataChannels.

• Automatic Camera Roll Backup
Back up your iPhone and iPad photos and videos directly to your home computer, NAS, or router drive in original full resolution.

• Built-in Local Device Drive & BYOS Gateways
Use your iPhone or iPad right out of the box as a portable P2P drive, pair desktop/server nodes in seconds via QR code or Link ID, or connect to your own self-hosted gateway.

• Personal Knowledge Base (RAG) & Private AI Assistant
Connect your own self-hosted AI node to automatically index your photos and documents into a searchable visual knowledge base, or use the built-in public demo assistant for document Q&A, chat, and creative studio illustrations.

• Rich Media & Office Document Suite
Preview high-resolution photo galleries, stream video and audio, and view or edit Markdown, code, spreadsheets, presentations, and documents directly in the app.

• Decentralized Cryptographic Identity (DID)
Sign in with Apple, Google, or a self-custodial cryptographic keypair. Paired device links and preferences sync end-to-end encrypted across your devices.

Self-hosting guides & node installation for Linux, Windows, macOS, Docker, and OpenWrt: https://docs.disk.direct
```

### What's New in This Version
