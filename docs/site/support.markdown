## 3. Account Management, Privacy & Data Deletion

**On My Disk** is built on zero-custody peer-to-peer architecture:

* **Your Files Stay on Your Hardware**: Files shared from your paired devices stream directly peer-to-peer over encrypted **WebRTC DataChannels** (`DTLS/SCTP`) and are never stored in third-party cloud buckets.
* **In-App Account Deletion**: You can permanently delete your account and associated profile metadata at any time directly inside the mobile or web app under **Settings → Profile → Danger Zone → Delete Account**.
* **Support-Assisted Deletion & GDPR Requests**: If you no longer have access to your device, email [**info@onmydisk.com**](mailto:info@onmydisk.com) or open a support request in our [**Discord Support Server**](https://discord.gg/qAQpfmrBPW). Verified deletion and data-rights requests are fulfilled promptly in accordance with GDPR.
* **Legal & Privacy Documentation**: Read our full **[Terms of Service & Privacy Policy (GDPR)](https://onmychat.com/tos#gdpr)**.
