# 🌐 About **On My Disk** & **On My Chat**
<hr>

**On My Disk Direct** (**On My Disk 3.0**) and **On My Chat** are open-source (`Apache-2.0`), community-driven projects built around a straightforward mission: give individuals and teams modern cloud convenience and frontier AI productivity **without surrendering ownership of their files, identity, or private knowledge**.

## Core Architectural Principles

* **Zero Cloud File Custody**: Files remain on your own disks (Linux servers, SBCs, Windows PCs, mobile devices, and browser peers) and stream directly peer-to-peer over **WebRTC DataChannels** (`libdatachannel` / SCTP-over-DTLS).
* **Self-Sovereign Identity (`W3C DID`)**: Accounts are anchored in client-side cryptographic keypairs (`did:gun:<pub>` on **GunDB SEA**) with delegated device certificates and end-to-end encrypted chat/knowledge graphs.
* **Local Knowledge, Flexible Compute**: With **Private Multimodal RAG** and **Agentic P2P Mode**, even a lightweight GPU-less laptop keeps its embeddings and **ChromaDB** vector index 100% local while seamlessly borrowing remote P2P GPU power for heavy LLM and **ComfyUI** generation.
